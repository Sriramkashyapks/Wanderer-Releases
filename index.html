How to Install on Android

3 simple steps to get Wanderer up and running on your Android phone.

1

Download APK

Tap the Download APK button above on your Android phone browser.

2

Allow Unknown Apps

When prompted by Chrome/Android, tap Settings and switch on "Allow from this source".

3

Open & Wander

Tap Install. Once completed, open Wanderer and start creating your first trip!
